## 🎯 PHASE 1: FIRST MONEY (Days 1-30)

**Objective:** Get 5-10 paying customers BEFORE public launch

### Week 1-2: "Friends & Family" Beta (Target: 5 beta customers)

**Strategy:** Personal network + give massive value for free in exchange for testimonials

**Action Plan:**

#### Day 1-3: Identify 20 Target Restaurants
```
✅ Your own city/locality (can visit in person)
✅ Small cafes/restaurants you've visited before
✅ Owners you know through friends/family
✅ Places where you're already a customer
```

**Outreach Script (WhatsApp/Call):**
```
"Hi [Name], I'm [You], a software developer. I've built a free 
restaurant POS system that works offline and has zero monthly fees. 

I'm giving it free to 5 restaurants for 6 months in exchange for 
feedback. Interested? I can set it up for you this weekend."
```

**What to offer:**
- ✅ Free for 6 months (no strings)
- ✅ Personal setup & training (you do it yourself)
- ✅ Your personal phone support
- ✅ After 6 months: ₹299/mo (50% discount forever)

**Why this works:**
- No risk for them (it's free)
- You get real users + testimonials
- You learn what breaks/what they need
- After 6 months, 70% will pay (they're already dependent on it)

#### Day 4-7: Setup First 5 Restaurants

**Time investment:** 4-6 hours per restaurant
- Install desktop app on their PC/tablet
- Import their menu (help them manually)
- Train staff (1 hour session)
- Leave printed quick-reference guide

**Get from each one:**
1. ✅ Video testimonial (30 seconds on phone)
2. ✅ "Before & After" story (what they used before, how RestroSuite is better)
3. ✅ Permission to use their logo/name
4. ✅ WhatsApp group with owner (for quick support)

**How it works:**

## 🚨 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### ❌ Don't Do This:

1. **Don't build new features before getting 10 customers**
   - You'll build what YOU think they need
   - Build what paying customers tell you they need

2. **Don't launch on ProductHunt/HackerNews first**
   - Huge traffic spike, then nothing
   - Get 10 paying customers first, THEN launch publicly

3. **Don't discount too much**
   - ₹299 beta is already cheap
   - Don't go below ₹199 (customers won't value it)

4. **Don't spend money on ads until ₹25k MRR**
   - Organic channels (FB groups, YouTube, referrals) are free
   - Only pay for ads when you have proven unit economics

5. **Don't try to sell to enterprises first**
   - They take 6-12 months to close
   - Start with small restaurants (sign up in 1 day)
